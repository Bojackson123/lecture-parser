# P4-03 — Gap carving, `Chunk`, `align_lecture()`, expected-chunks fixture
Phase 4 · Depends on: P4-02 · Size: L

## Goal

Finish stage 4 (plan §3): the `Chunk` type, the **gap signal** of plan §4.1 ("spans
where the lecturer talked for minutes with no matching slide content — that's board
work or live coding"), and the composing entrypoint
`align_lecture(deck, segments) -> [Chunk]`, mirroring `ingest_captions` and
`ingest_slides` as the only way anything else obtains chunks. The headline artifact is
a hand-written `tests/fixtures/align/lecture01.chunks.json` — the four chunks of the
fixtures README's slide → time map, whose spans are, by design, exactly the topic
anchors of the `week01` notes fixture (1–149, 151–268, 271–419, 421–545): the input
and output fixtures tell one story. The dice detour must come out as a `slides=None`
gap chunk; segment 4 (no slide vocabulary, but mid-flow) and segment 22 (the short
closing recap) must not.

3. **`Chunk`** (frozen pydantic, in `boundaries.py`): `slides: SlideRange | None`,
   `segments: tuple[Segment, ...]` (non-empty, validated); `start_s`/`end_s` as
   `@property` — `min(s.start_s)` / `max(s.end_s)` over members (spans are unions and
   may overlap; max, not last). Properties, not fields, so the JSON stays
   two-key and cannot disagree with the segments. `slides=None` **is** the gap
   signal; v1 always emits width-1 ranges, but the type is `SlideRange` now so the §9
   grouping decision won't churn it (the P3-01 `RenderResult.documents`-is-a-tuple
   precedent).
4. **Gap carving** (private helper in `boundaries.py`, tested through the
   entrypoint). Within each window, per unit: *off-slide* iff it shares **fewer than
   2** distinct scoring terms with the window's slide (`len(unit_terms &
   slide_terms) < 2` — tolerating the one stray hit P4-01 documented, segment 6's
   "number"). Take each maximal run of off-slide units, split it at **silences** —
   adjacent units whose spans are `min_silence_s` or more apart — and carve a piece
   out as a gap only when:
   - it is silence-bracketed on *both* sides (the lecture's very start and end count
     as brackets; the fixture's brackets are the 2 s at 149→151 and the 3 s at
     268→271, the latter documented in the fixtures README), **and**
   - it spans at least `min_gap_s` (§4.1 says "talked for *minutes*"; the detour's
     117 s clears the 60 s default, segment 22's 25 s does not).

   Pieces that fail either test stay in the slide's chunk. On the fixture the
   off-slide run is segments 4–12; the silence split peels segment 4 off (no leading
   silence — the lecturer was mid-flow) and carves exactly segments 5–12.
5. **`align_lecture(deck: Deck, segments: Sequence[Segment], *, min_gap_s: float =
   60.0, min_silence_s: float = 1.0) -> list[Chunk]`**, the only entrypoint:
   - no segments → `[]`;
   - candidates = non-hidden slides, in order (skip `hidden`, never renumber);
   - no candidates → one gap chunk holding everything;
   - otherwise: `span_units` → `term_weights` → per-unit term unions →
     `solve_windows` over the candidates' `slide_terms` → gap carving → chunks, in
     unit order: consecutive same-fate units within a window group into one chunk
     (`SlideRange(n, n)` for the window's slide, `None` for a carved piece); a carved
     middle therefore splits a window into slide / gap / slide, and the same range
     may appear twice — monotonicity is non-decreasing, not strict. Empty windows
     produce no chunk.
6. **`tests/fixtures/README.md`**: add

   to the listing, and under the slide → time map note that the JSON is its

## Decisions & notes

- **Gaps need silence brackets *and* minutes, not just missing vocabulary.** Speech
  that merely lacks slide words is everywhere — transitions ("back to the slides"),
  asides, segment 4's foreshadowing — and it belongs to the slide on screen. What
  distinguishes board work is the lecturer *leaving*: a pause at each end and a span
  measured in minutes. The fixture encodes exactly this (its only ≥ 1 s silences
  bracket the detour), and P1's span-union rule is what makes the silences visible to
  alignment at all — the anchor honesty invariant paying rent.
- **The off-slide test is `< 2` shared terms, not `score == 0`.** One coincidental
  content word (the gap's "number" against slide 1's "a number received…") must not
  disqualify a gap; two distinct shared informative terms is real slide talk. A unit
  sharing one heavy term ("bellman" alone) would count off-slide too — and then be
  saved by the silence/duration gates; acceptable, and property-tested only through
  the gates.
- **Gap chunks are content, not noise.** `slides=None` is the §4.1 gap signal: Phase
  5 generates notes from these chunks like any other (the `week01` fixture already
  has the board-work topic with a slide-less anchor), and Phase 9 uses them as the
  trigger for frame pulls. Nothing may drop them.
- **Hidden slides are excluded before the DP**, not after: a hidden slide must not
  soak up units it can never own in the output. Their numbers stay untouched
  (`Slide.number` is the file position — P2 invariant), so a chunk can never cite a
  slide the reader can't find.
- **A deck with no visible slides yields one ungated gap chunk.** There is nothing to
  attach speech to and no boundary evidence to gate on; the alternative (raising)
  would make a captions-only lecture unalignable, which Phase 5 will want (§1: "any
  mix" of materials).
- **Chunk spans may overlap and do not partition time** — inherited directly from the
  P1-03 span-union rule; sort by `start_s`, never assume adjacency. The chunks
  *partition the segments*, which is the plan §10 property, and the fixture's chunk
  spans have visible holes (149→151) where the silences live.
- **`align_lecture` returns `list[Chunk]`, plain.** No wrapper `Alignment` type until
  something needs one; the §4.1 gap signal is already in-band as `slides=None`, and
  unmatched slides are recoverable by comparing the deck against the ranges used.
